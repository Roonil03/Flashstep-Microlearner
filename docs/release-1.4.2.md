# Release 1.4.2

Flashstep Microlearner 1.4.2 gives each part of a study session a recognizable color in light and dark mode.

## Finding your way

- Home leads with a deck title and its due status. Tap the title to open that deck, or use **Start review** below today's available and reviewed counts to choose a deck for a session. Other decks appear in a compact library list.
- Inside a study session, cobalt identifies the question and green identifies the answer. Deck management, progress and account screens also pair color with text labels.
- Sign-in and registration use centered forms on a coral background. Registration uses the opposite light or dark theme, so it is easy to tell which form is open. The forms scroll above the keyboard.

## Decks and review

- Open a personal deck by its title on Home. Each title has the card and due counts underneath. Search the library by title, or open **Browse public decks** to find an editable copy for your account.
- Deck details put **Start review**, the card editor and **Import CSV** in the same workflow. You can see the remaining card capacity before confirming an import.
- During review, the question stays on the page when you reveal the answer on a separate green sheet. Each rating has a symbol, a name and a short explanation. The brief ripple on **Show answer** respects reduced motion.
- The nine-step guide previews the new controls. Replay it from **Settings → Relearn app navigation**.
- Home distinguishes finishing the day's allowance from reviewing every due card. The daily limit applies when selective review is enabled. **Review all cards** overrides the limit.

## Progress and preferences

- Headings and body text use smaller base sizes in both themes. The four font-size choices in System Settings still adjust the app relative to your device's text size.
- Analytics groups activity, retention and card workload with the selected date range and snapshot time. Activity and retention charts sit alongside each other on wider layouts and stack on phones.
- Expand a chart’s daily values to read the review count or retention for a particular date. Each date is also available individually to assistive technology.
- In Settings, tap your name to open **Account Settings**, or **System Settings** to change your theme, font size and review limits. **Help & information** groups the navigation guide, legal notices and public bug reporting in one list. Long legal notices remain readable offline.
- Moving between Settings sections keeps your name and review preferences in place. Back returns to your current dashboard, and saved changes update the relevant information on Home.
- On small screens with enlarged text, deck and card save buttons grow to fit their labels. The deck creation action remains reachable while the keyboard is open.

## Compatibility

This interface update uses the existing deck library, review history, sync API, and database schema.
