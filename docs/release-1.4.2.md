# Release 1.4.2

Flashstep Microlearner 1.4.2 gives each part of a study session a recognizable color in light and dark mode.

## Finding your way

- DM Sans brings consistent typography to study screens, forms, settings and navigation. The font is bundled for offline use, and its license is available under **Settings → Legal → Open-source licenses → DM Sans**.

- Home leads with a deck title and its due status. Tap the title to open that deck, or use **Start review** to choose a deck for a session. **Total Decks** counts your whole library, including the featured deck.
- Inside a study session, cobalt identifies the question and green identifies the answer. Deck management, progress and account screens also pair color with text labels.
- Sign-in and registration display the Flashstep logo above centered forms on a coral background. Registration uses the opposite light or dark theme, so it is easy to tell which form is open. The forms scroll above the keyboard.

## Decks and review

- Open a personal deck by its title on Home. Each title has the card and due counts underneath. Search the library by title, or open **Browse public decks** to find an editable copy for your account.
- Deck details put **Start review**, the card editor and **Import CSV** in the same workflow. You can see the remaining card capacity before confirming an import.
- During review, the question stays on the page when you reveal the answer on a separate green sheet. Each rating has a symbol, a name and a short explanation. The brief ripple on **Show answer** respects reduced motion.
- The nine-step guide previews the new controls. Replay it from **Settings → Relearn app navigation**.
- Home distinguishes finishing the day's allowance from reviewing every due card. The daily limit applies when selective review is enabled. **Review all cards** overrides the limit.

## Progress and preferences

- A cloud button beside Analytics shows sync progress and whether your device is working offline. Tap it to retry, or sign in again when your session needs renewing.
- Opening the app offline restores your saved account and its local decks. Sign Out becomes available after a successful sync with no pending changes, so you can reconnect before leaving the account.
- Settings displays the Flashstep logo with its name. Uninstalling the Android app removes its local library. App data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer.

- In System Settings, **Device default** now matches the previous **Large** size. All four presets follow your device's text-size setting.
- Analytics groups activity, retention and card workload with the selected date range and snapshot time. Activity and retention charts sit alongside each other on wider layouts and stack on phones.
- Expand a chart’s daily values to read the review count or retention for a particular date. Each date is also available individually to assistive technology.
- In Settings, tap your name to open **Account Settings**, or **System Settings** to change your theme, font size and review limits. **Report a Bug** is under **Help & information**. **Legal** contains the policies and open-source licenses. The centered red **Sign Out** button sits above the full-color Flashstep logo and installed version.
- Moving between Settings sections keeps your name and review preferences in place. Back returns to your current dashboard, and saved changes update the relevant information on Home.
- The bug report form asks where the problem happened, what you saw, and what you expected. You can expand an optional field for steps to reproduce it. Before posting, the app asks you to confirm that your report can be public.
- If the reporting bot cannot send your report, the form keeps your text and offers a link to a new GitHub issue. You can copy your report across and publish it from your own GitHub account.
- On small screens with enlarged text, deck and card save buttons grow to fit their labels. The deck creation action remains reachable while the keyboard is open.

## Compatibility

This interface update uses the existing deck library, review history, sync API, and database schema.
