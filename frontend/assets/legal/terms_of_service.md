# Terms of Service

Placeholder information for Flashstep Microlearner. This page describes the intended service terms but is not a binding agreement. The service operator, contact details, effective date, dispute process, and other legal terms have not been finalized.

## 1. Future terms and acceptance

Account creation while this placeholder is displayed does not accept any future Terms of Service. Before binding terms are introduced, Flashstep will identify the operator and effective date, present the complete terms and a notice of material changes, and request appropriate affirmative acceptance. Existing users will not be treated as having automatically agreed to new terms through earlier account creation or continued use alone. If acceptance is required to keep using a feature, the app will explain that choice when the final terms are presented.

## 2. What the app does

Flashstep lets people create and import flashcard decks, review cards, browse and copy public decks, sync study data between devices, and view analytics. Offline changes can remain on a device until a successful sync. There is currently no in-app payment or subscription flow.

## 3. Accounts and shared content

Accounts use a username, email address, and password. People can make decks public so other signed-in users can browse and make personal copies. Users should share only content they have permission to share and avoid placing private information in public decks. Final rules about eligibility, content moderation, suspension, and public copies have not been set.

## 4. Availability and account deletion

Connectivity and maintenance can interrupt the service. Account Settings offers an account deletion request. The app removes the local account database on the device used, while server cleanup is scheduled to remove deleted accounts later. Signing out leaves local study data on the device.

No liability cap, arbitration requirement, or class-action waiver is imposed by this placeholder. Mandatory rights under applicable law remain available.
